# v7 proposal: feedback register

Every comment on the v7 proposal gets an identifier here, whatever its source, so that each change to the draft can say which comment it answers. Comments given in another language are recorded in an English translation. A comment is closed only when its source agrees.

Status: `open` (not yet answered), `drafted` (answered in the draft, waiting for the source), `closed` (the source agrees), `declined` (with the reason), `deferred` (to a later version, with the reason).

## Decisions on stage placement

Taken by Radosław Taraszka on 5 October 2026 in answer to F1 and F2. Jacek Siadkowski has not reviewed them yet.

| ID | Decision | In the draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Every stage opens with the same block: what places an organization at the stage, what it requires (without it, the stage is lower), and a readiness checklist whose gaps never lower the stage. §2 summarizes the five stages in one table. | §2, Dimension 1; §5, Placement in each stage |
| P2 | Stage 1 is placed by use, not attitude: the organization does not use AI in its work, and nobody owns its adoption. Skepticism and concerns describe a typical Stage 1 organization; they are not a condition. | §5, Stage 1 |
| P3 | Stage 2 is placed by any use of AI in people's own work, however uneven. | §5, Stage 2 |
| P4 | Stage 3 requires, besides the named owner, champion, or adoption group, that people share what works and what fails across the organization. This can move an organization from Stage 3 to Stage 2. | §5, Stage 3 |
| P5 | Stage 5 requires everything Stage 4 requires, other organizations depending on the capability with demonstrated value, sustainable funding, and maintenance and incident capacity. The other guardrails become a readiness checklist. v6 required all twelve guardrails, so this relaxes v6. | §5, Stage 5; refined by P9 |
| P6 | §10, Recommended Stage Transitions, is removed: its lists repeat the stage profiles. Its identifiers are retired, with a map to where each item lives in v7. | §10 removed; the map is in `v7.md`, Retired identifiers |

## Decisions on Jacek Siadkowski's review

Taken by Radosław Taraszka on 9 October 2026 in answer to F4–F6. Jacek Siadkowski has not reviewed them yet. Parts marked _proposed_ are not decided: they wait for the open questions in `v7.md`.

| ID | Decision | In the draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P7 | Stage 3 also requires S3.def.1: AI use becomes expected in selected areas rather than exceptional. _Proposed:_ one team or function where AI is part of how the work is normally done is enough (open question 3). This can move an organization from Stage 3 to Stage 2. | §2; §5, Stage 3 |
| P8 | Stage 4 placement is one table: S4.def.1 places the organization; S4.def.2–6, S4.gate.3 and S4.gate.5 are required, in five rows. "AI is deployed and governed in a crucial internal or important beneficiary-facing workflow" summarizes them; the failure test defines crucial and important. Governed does not include the gates for the people served. Nobody's stage changes. | §2; §5, Stage 4 |
| P9 | Stage 5 is reached when independent organizations use, in their own work, a capability the organization created (S5.def.2, S5.guardrail.1); _proposed:_ at least two of them (open question 4). It requires that they obtain demonstrated value from it (S5.def.3) and that sustainable funding and maintenance and incident capacity cover it (S5.guardrail.3, S5.guardrail.5). The organization's own answer is accepted if it names the organizations. Compared with the 5 October draft, an organization with one user, or with funding that does not cover the capability, is at Stage 4. | §2; §5, Stage 5 |
| P10 | Every placement table gives, for each row, the v6 identifier and a question that checks it. Assessment tools may word the question their own way. | §2; §5 |
| P11 | _Proposed:_ an organization where someone owns AI adoption but nobody uses AI yet is at Stage 2. | §2; §5, Stages 1 and 2 |

The Stage 3 "reached when" wording (pilots on selected workflows, or the Adoption Loop run more than once) is drafted from What Defines This Stage and has no decision behind it yet.
